# Walk every screen the way a player reaches it, then read the sheet. Run:
#   rbxgame tour scenarios/ui-tour.txt                  (desktop)
#   rbxgame tour scenarios/ui-tour.txt --device "iPhone 1"   (phone, landscape)
# Steps: play | stop | wait <s> | shot <name> | click <path under PlayerGui> | client <Luau> | server <Luau>
# Studio's simulated clicks can miss on a simulated phone, so each click is followed by the same intent fired
# from the client; the server ignores the duplicate. Paths come from src/client/Hud.luau (ScreenGui "Hud").
play
wait 8
shot title
click Hud.Root.Title.Face.Play
client game.ReplicatedStorage.Action:FireServer("Start")
wait 1.5
shot play
client for _ = 1, 3 do game.ReplicatedStorage.Action:FireServer("Act", 0) task.wait(0.4) end
wait 3
shot results
click Hud.Root.Results.Face.Again
client game.ReplicatedStorage.Action:FireServer("Start")
wait 1.5
shot play-again
client for _ = 1, 3 do game.ReplicatedStorage.Action:FireServer("Act", 0) task.wait(0.4) end
wait 2
click Hud.Root.Results.Face.Menu
client game.ReplicatedStorage.Action:FireServer("Menu")
wait 2
shot title-again
